FAQ

Questions we hear often

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. Tell us what you need through the contact form and we will send a clear quote after a short call.

How long does a WordPress website take?

A simple business site is usually quicker than a custom store or web app. We give you a timeline once the scope is agreed.

Can you manage my website after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, updates, backups, and support after the project goes live.

Do you help with SEO and ads too?

Yes. Our digital marketing service covers SEO, social media, and paid campaigns, either with a new site or an existing one.

Will I own the website and the code?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the website, files, and accounts are yours.
